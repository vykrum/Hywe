About Hywe

HYWE

Hygrid Woven Ensemble — Computational Spatial Design & Deterministic Layout Engine

A relational and topology-driven spatial design environment for architects, urban designers, and researchers. Instead of manually drafting geometric shapes or relying on black-box generative imagery, you define the functional flow and spatial relationships between spaces, and HYWE's deterministic layout engine weaves them into resolved planar floor layouts and 3D volumetric massing in real time.

Core Capabilities

Spatial Program & Node Editor

Build and edit architectural spatial hierarchies visually with interactive tree graphs, or write compact alphanumeric definitions.

Deterministic 2D Floor Layouts

Real-time topological space packing with site boundary confinement, room adjacency alignment, and instant SVG export.

3D Volumetric Stacking

Multi-level vertical spatial massing accelerated directly in the browser via modern WebGPU rendering.

Community Gallery

Browse, filter, and load curated community designs and spatial solutions directly from Hugging Face into your active canvas.

Files & Sharing

Export and import standalone .hyw design files, load built-in topological presets from Waggle Exchange, and share or bookmark configurations with full URL state persistence.

Teach & Dataset Contribution

Pair natural-language architectural intents with spatial geometries to generate structured datasets for layout-aware AI models.

Scope & Design Philosophy

HYWE is an experimental research apparatus and standalone exploration environment for studying relational space planning and deterministic layout generation. Its objective is to investigate how complex architectural spatial configurations can emerge deterministically from topological rules, circulation flow, and discrete integer lattices.
