HYWE — Computational Spatial Design Environment

HYWE is a computational spatial design environment for generating and exploring architectural layouts during early-stage design. It converts spatial programs, relationships, hierarchy and constraints into discrete spatial configurations that can be generated, compared and explored before detailed geometric modelling.

Relational spatial design

HYWE represents architectural programs through spaces, relationships, hierarchy, sequence and constraints. The system generates spatial configurations from these discrete relationships before detailed geometric modelling.

Computational architectural layout generation

HYWE provides a browser-based environment for procedural space planning, topology-driven layout generation and computational exploration of architectural configurations.
